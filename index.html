Design Beginnings


Design Fuego

Used HTML and CSS to create a simple and colorful introduction website.

Logo A/B/C

Tasked with creating a logo that represents me professionally and as a person. Used generative AI to ideate on the first two designs, then completed the third and final prototype traditionally on canva.

UX Testing Logo Project

Interviewed 3 different subjects and got feedback for the prototype logo. Utilized said feedback to make necessary changes to the logo, then condensed my findings and results into a PowerPoint presentation.

Good Design

Used Adobe InDesign to create a graphic that analyzes the design elements of a piece of my choosing (Invincible 70 cover). Learned how to more effectively and comfortably wield the software.

Course Goals


1. Consider the role of rhetoric, technology, and ethics in acts of making.

When it comes to the ethics of the design process, the most important thing to consider currently is the role of generative AI. I encountered this when initially creating my logo prototypes. Using generative AI feels like cheating to me, especially in a visual piece. When I notice that an advertisement or graphic has been made with AI, I can feel myself pay less attention to it. AI is a great tool for drafting, but should never> be used to produce a final product.

2. Leverage Design Principles.

I had the most practice with leveraging design principles in project 4. I wanted to make sure I properly complied with the rules of alignment. I really enjoyed using the color palette tool and experimenting with different background/title color combos to create contrast.

3. Engage with others in a workshop environment.

The weekly small group workshops have been useful not only for receiving feedback on my own work, but for seeing what other people are working on. I find that the quickest way to get inspiration during a creative endeavor is to look at cool things that others have made.



4. Practice revision within and across media forms.

While working on projects 2 and 3, I was constantly revising and reinventing my logo with a multitude of different tools. I started with generative AI for the low-fidelity prototypes, then switched to Canva and InDesign for my later designs. The final design I went with was made in Canva.

5. Learn to present your work.

In addition to the small group workshops, I had an opportunity to practice my public speaking skills in the UX study project. Public speaking is not my favorite thing to do, but I understand its importance in the creative process.
